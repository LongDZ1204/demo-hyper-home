Ear Piercing Services in San Antonio

Ear piercing services in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers cover lobe piercings, cartilage piercings, curated ears, and first piercings for children, the four listed below. Each is split by what you come in for rather than by where it sits on the ear.

  1. Lobe piercing in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers gives first-time and everyday clients a clean, versatile base for classic studs, paired lobes, or stacked-lobe styling. A lobe suits you when you want a straightforward first piercing, or another hole to build a stack on later.

    The lobe placements our piercers work cover:

    • The single lobe
    • A second and third above it
    • A stacked run up the lobe
    • Paired lobes done in the one visit

  2. Cartilage piercing in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers adds shape and detail to the upper ear, with more visible styling options than the lobe. Cartilage suits you when you want a sharper accent, a balanced upper-ear detail, or the base for a future curated ear.

    The placements our piercers work run from:

    • Helix, mid-helix, and hidden helix
    • Forward helix
    • Conch
    • Flat
    • Tragus and antitragus
    • Rook
    • Daith
    • Industrial

  3. Ear curation in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers turns separate lobe and cartilage piercings into one balanced ear layout rather than a set of holes added at random. Curation suits you when you already have a piercing or two and want the next ones planned around them. Your piercer maps the spacing against your ear anatomy, fits starter jewelry that holds the look while it settles, and stages which placements to do now and which to add later.

    Walk in with a few inspiration photos, and a piercer will tell you what fits your ear.

  4. Kids ear piercing in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers is a child's first lobe done with a single-use needle rather than a piercing gun, with a parent alongside and ID checked at the front desk. A gun forces a blunt stud through the lobe under spring pressure, while a needle makes a clean opening the jewelry passes into, so children are pierced on the same equipment as everyone else.

Why Choose Hyper Inkers for Your Ear Piercing in San Antonio

The advantages of choosing Hyper Inkers for ear piercing in San Antonio are one team for an ear you build over years, cartilage staged so the ear does not get crowded, free aftercare check-ins for the whole heal, jewelry inside the price rather than added to it, and walk-in access with no appointment and no deposit, the five listed below. An ear is rarely finished in one visit, so the studio you pick is the one that lives with the layout.

  1. An ear gets built one placement at a time, often across years, and Hyper Inkers keeps that history with the piercers who made it. Your next hole is marked against the ones already in your ear rather than against a chart, so the spacing still holds as the layout grows.

  2. Cartilage takes months rather than weeks, so your piercer stages it instead of stacking it in one sitting. You get told what to take today and what to come back for, even when you walked in ready for both, because two fresh holes side by side are harder to sleep on and slower to settle.

  3. Free aftercare check-ins run for as long as the piercing takes to settle, at any stage, and sterile saline is sold at the front desk if you run out. Cartilage is the placement that needs them, since it is the one you live with longest before it stops being a wound.

  4. Jewelry sits inside the price of every ear piercing rather than being rung up on top of it, and so does the downsize your piercer fits once the first piece has done its job. Studios that quote a piercing cheap and sell the metal separately end up costing more by the time you walk out.

  5. Ear piercings are walk-in only, so there is no appointment to book and no deposit to put down. The studio stays open until midnight Monday through Thursday and until 1am Friday through Sunday, and a lobe takes minutes, so it fits after work as easily as on a Saturday.

Ear Piercing Jewelry

Ear piercing jewelry at Hyper Inkers in San Antonio is implant-grade titanium, a body-safe metal with no nickel in it, so a fresh piercing has nothing to react to while the skin closes around the post. The piece that goes in first is picked for how it heals rather than how it photographs, and the styling comes after that.

  • The starter piece goes in long, leaving room for the swelling that follows any piercing, then your piercer swaps it for a shorter post once that drops.
  • Lobes and cartilage take studs, flat-back studs, hoops, or barbells, but which one goes in first is your piercer's call based on the placement rather than the look you walked in with.
  • You are welcome to bring a piece you already own, as long as it is implant grade and the right length for a fresh piercing.

A flat-back stud is the default on cartilage, because a hoop drags against the healing channel every time you sleep on that side.

Ear Piercing FAQ

Common questions about getting an ear piercing at Hyper Inkers are answered below.

Ear piercings run $30 to $60 by placement, with basic titanium jewelry included, and every placement drops during Happy Hour, which takes a lobe to $15.

Yes, ear piercings are walk-in only, so just stop in any day, no appointment needed.

Yes, implant-grade titanium starter jewelry is included with every ear piercing.

Hurt is a big word for it, since what you feel is a quick, sharp pinch, and the lobe hurts least of any placement. Cartilage placements like the helix, tragus, or industrial feel sharper because the tissue is firmer. Pain still varies by person, so no ear piercing is truly pain-free.

A lobe usually heals in about 6 to 8 weeks. Cartilage takes longer, often 6 months to a year, because it has less blood flow. Healing times vary, so follow the aftercare your piercer gives you.

Caring for a new ear piercing means sterile saline twice a day, the jewelry left where it is, and no twisting it or sleeping on that side. Stay out of pools and hot tubs while it heals, and ask your piercer if anything looks off. Read what slows a new piercing down while it heals.

No, not with sea salt you mix at home. The aftercare Hyper Inkers sends you home with follows the Association of Professional Piercers standard, which is sterile saline wound wash at 0.9% sodium chloride, twice a day, and no twisting the jewelry. Salt water mixed in a kitchen is easy to get wrong on concentration and is not sterile, so it either dries the piercing out or brings something new to it. Sterile saline is sold at the front desk if you run out.

The safest place is one that pierces with a needle, opens it where you can see, and can show you how its tools get sterilized. Hyper Inkers takes the needle out of a sealed pack at the chair, changes gloves between steps, and sends reusable tools through an autoclave that goes to a lab for spore testing on a schedule. Ask to see any of that and your piercer will walk you through it.

Hyper Inkers pierces with a fresh single-use needle, never a piercing gun. A gun is plastic, so it cannot be put through an autoclave the way steel tools can, and it drives a blunt stud through the tissue instead of making the clean opening a needle makes.

Yes, you are welcome to bring a piece you already own, as long as it is implant grade and the right length for a fresh piercing.
