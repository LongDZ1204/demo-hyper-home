Ear Piercing Services in San Antonio

Ear piercing services in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers cover lobe piercings, cartilage piercings, and curated ears, for a clean first piercing, a more visible upper-ear detail, or a planned ear layout. We split ear work into three so you can pick by what you want, not by guesswork. A lobe is the easiest clean starting point, cartilage adds sharper detail higher on the ear, and a curated ear plans several placements as one look.

  1. Lobe piercing in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers gives first-time and everyday clients a clean, versatile base for classic studs, paired lobes, or stacked-lobe styling. A lobe is the simplest ear piercing to style and to pair, and a natural first step before cartilage.

    Common lobe placements include:

    • Single lobe
    • Second or third lobe
    • Stacked lobe
    • Double lobe

    Lobe piercings usually heal faster than cartilage, and they start at $15 with jewelry included.

    Explore lobe piercing

  2. Cartilage piercing in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers adds shape and detail to the upper ear, with more visible styling options than the lobe. Cartilage works well when you want a sharper accent, a balanced upper-ear detail, or the base for a future curated ear.

    The cartilage placements we pierce in San Antonio include:

    • Helix and mid-helix
    • Forward helix
    • Conch
    • Flat
    • Tragus and antitragus
    • Rook
    • Daith
    • Industrial

    Cartilage gives more placement options than the lobe and takes longer to heal. Cartilage piercing in San Antonio starts at $15, with the tragus and most upper-ear placements at $20 and an industrial at $30. Jewelry is included.

    Explore cartilage piercing

  3. Ear curation in San Antonio at Hyper Inkers turns separate lobe and cartilage piercings into a balanced ear layout, with better spacing, jewelry fit, and staged placement. A curated ear plans three things:

    • Placement map. Your piercer maps your existing holes, ear anatomy, and open space so the layout stays balanced instead of crowded, across placements like a stacked lobe, helix, conch, or tragus.
    • Jewelry fit. Starter jewelry supports the look without fighting your anatomy or early healing, and you upgrade the style once it settles.
    • Healing sequence. Not every placement should be done at once, so your piercer can suggest what to pierce now and what to add later.

    Walk in with a few inspiration photos, and a piercer will tell you what fits your ear.

An ear piercing feels like a quick, sharp pinch, and it heals in a few weeks for a lobe or several months for cartilage. How much it hurts and how long it takes depend mostly on the placement, so here is what to expect before you walk in.

A lobe is the mildest ear piercing. Cartilage placements like the helix, tragus, or industrial feel sharper because the tissue is firmer. Pain still varies by person, so no ear piercing is truly pain-free.

A lobe usually heals in about 6 to 8 weeks. Cartilage takes longer, often 6 months to a year, because it has less blood flow. Healing times vary, so follow the aftercare your piercer gives you.

Clean it with sterile saline once or twice a day, leave the jewelry in, and avoid twisting it or sleeping on that side. Stay out of pools and hot tubs while it heals, and ask your piercer if anything looks off. Read the full ear piercing aftercare and healing guides.
