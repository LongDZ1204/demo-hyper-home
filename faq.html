Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tattoos, cover-ups, piercings, and laser tattoo removal at Hyper Inkers Tattoo Studio in San Antonio.

Booking & Studio

Tattoos are by appointment; piercings are walk-ins only. For a tattoo, message us with your idea, size, and placement so we can match you with the right artist and quote you. For a piercing, walk in during open hours or call (210) 997-9737.

We accept cash, Zelle, and Cash App.

Tattoo

Tattoos are priced by the hour based on size, detail, style, and placement. At Hyper Inkers, rates start around $80/hr (minimum), average about $150/hr, and go up to $200/hr for our top artists. The best way to get an exact quote is an in-person consultation. You can also see our guide on tattoo prices in San Antonio.

The surface heals in about 2 to 3 weeks, but full healing of the deeper skin takes 4 to 6 weeks. Keep it clean and moisturized, and avoid sun and soaking during that time.

You must be 18 or older to get tattooed at Hyper Inkers. Texas law restricts tattooing anyone under 18, so if you are a minor, please contact us with a parent or legal guardian to discuss your options before booking. If it is your first tattoo, read our guide on things to know before your first tattoo.

It is generally not recommended to get tattooed while pregnant or breastfeeding because of the small risk of infection. Most artists, including ours, advise waiting until after. When in doubt, check with your doctor first.

Pain tolerance differs from person to person and varies by location on the body. Most people describe it as manageable scratching or burning. See our tattoo pain chart for which spots hurt most and least.

Tipping is not required but is appreciated. A common range is 15 to 20% of the total, and more for complex or custom work. Cash is always welcome.

Eat beforehand, stay hydrated, and dress so the artist can easily reach the placement area.

Wash with warm water and antibacterial soap, pat dry, and apply a thin layer of antibiotic cream or lotion 3 to 5 times a day for the first week. Do not scratch or peel scabs, do not soak in water, and avoid the sun. Keep moisturizing until fully healed (about 4 to 6 weeks). Touch-ups can be done once healed.

Avoid touching, picking, or scratching it; submerging it in water (no swimming, baths, or lakes); direct sunlight; and heavy drinking right after, since it thins the blood. These all slow healing or risk infection. See our full checklist on what to avoid after getting a tattoo.

Some redness and swelling is normal for a few days. Warning signs of infection are spreading redness, warmth, pus or oozing, and worsening pain after day 3. If you see those, contact us and see a doctor.

Cover-Up Tattoo

A cover-up tattoo is a new design built to conceal or transform an existing tattoo you no longer want, using placement, shading, and darker tones so it looks intentional. Browse our custom work to see the styles we cover up in.

Most can, depending on the size, age, darkness, and placement of the original. Very dark or solid tattoos usually need a larger or darker design, and sometimes a few fading sessions first.

Not always. Lighter or older tattoos can often be covered directly; very dark pieces cover better after a few fading sessions. Your artist assesses this at the consultation.

Cover-ups use the same hourly rate as custom tattoos but often take more time because of the extra design work. You will get an accurate quote after a consultation.

Piercing

Piercing prices at Hyper Inkers start at $30 and depend on the placement, and basic jewelry is included. As a guide: earlobes are $30; nose and most cartilage piercings like helix and conch are $35; eyebrow, navel, and nipple are $40; septum, tragus, and labret are $50; industrial and dermal are $60; and surface piercings up to $70. Call (210) 997-9737 for current pricing. Ask about Happy Hour pricing, which runs lower on select hours, on our current deals.

It depends on the type. Earlobes generally heal in 6 to 8 weeks, while cartilage piercings can take 6 months to a year. Aftercare is essential to heal properly and avoid complications.

Wait 24 hours after getting pierced, then clean it twice a day with a sterile saline spray. Do not use alcohol, peroxide, or harsh soaps, and do not twist the jewelry. Always wash your hands first.

A small bump is a common irritation, not always an infection. Keep up your saline cleanings, avoid touching or changing the jewelry, and make sure it is not too tight. If it grows, leaks, or hurts, come see us or a piercer before trying home remedies.

Wait until the piercing is fully healed before changing jewelry, about 6 to 8 weeks for lobes and several months for cartilage. Changing too early can irritate the piercing or reopen the wound. If unsure, stop by and we will check it.

Signs of infection include spreading redness, swelling, warmth, and yellow or green discharge. Keep cleaning with saline, do not remove the jewelry yourself (that can trap the infection), and see us or a doctor if it worsens.

It depends on your tolerance and the placement. For most people it is a brief, sharp pinch that fades quickly.

No. Wait until it is fully healed. Pools, lakes, and hot tubs carry bacteria that raise infection risk and slow healing.

It is not recommended while nursing, due to infection risk and the medications that may be needed if a complication occurs. It is best to wait until after.

Do not touch it with dirty hands, twist the jewelry, use harsh products, submerge it in water, apply ointments, change jewelry too early, sleep on it, or expose it to friction.

Laser Tattoo Removal

Laser tattoo removal at Hyper Inkers is priced per session by the size of the tattoo, from about $100 to $150 for a stamp-size piece up to $600+ for a full sleeve or back. Because most tattoos need several sessions, complete removal commonly runs $1,000 to $4,000 total. Multi-session packages are available at a lower per-session rate, and you get a personalized estimate at a free consultation. (Removal is cosmetic, so it is not covered by insurance, though HSA and FSA funds may apply.)

A laser breaks the tattoo ink into tiny particles that your body's immune system clears away over the following weeks. At Hyper Inkers we use a PicoStar picosecond laser protocol.

Yes. Laser removal can fully clear most tattoos over a series of sessions. Black ink fades fastest; some colors and older or amateur tattoos take longer.

There is some discomfort, often described like a rubber band snapping against the skin, but each session is quick and numbing options are available.

Each session is short, usually a few minutes, but you will need several, spaced a few weeks apart so the skin can recover and the ink fades between visits. Smaller tattoos average 4 to 7 sessions, while larger or densely-inked pieces take 8 to 15. The exact number depends on size, age, colors, and ink depth, and your tech estimates it at your consultation.

When the laser is set correctly and you follow aftercare, scarring is uncommon. Temporary lightening or darkening of the skin can happen but usually evens out as it heals.

It is not recommended during pregnancy. It is best to wait until after, and to mention any medications or conditions at your consultation.

Keep the area clean and dry, apply a thin layer of healing ointment, protect it from the sun, and avoid swimming, soaking, and heavy sweating until healed.

Yes, once the skin has fully healed and the tone has settled. Many clients fade a dark tattoo over a few sessions so the cover-up looks cleaner and brighter.
