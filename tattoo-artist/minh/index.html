More About Minh Pham

Background, working style, and fit notes for clients who want to understand the artist before booking.

  1. Pham Minh Phuc, known as Minh, is the founder of Hyper Inkers, voted Best Tattoo Shop and Piercing Studio in San Antonio by San Antonio Current readers in 2026. He began tattooing in Vietnam at the age of 16 and has spent more than 10 years developing his craft.

    He studied graphic design before committing fully to tattooing and later built his career in San Antonio.

  2. Since then, Minh has focused on rigorous self-learning and mentorship from talented local artists, building a strong foundation in his craft. Working alongside other skilled artists continues to push his creative growth.

    He is deeply committed to development as an artist, shown through consistent self-study, adaptability, and active participation in competitions.

  3. Minh specializes in Black and Grey Realism and has earned 10 Best of Show titles across international competitions. He also works in Color Realism and holds a deep appreciation for Japanese-inspired tattoos.

  4. Minh is also a certified laser tattoo removal technician at Hyper Inkers, trained as a Laser Specialist at New Look Laser College and in tattoo removal at the Texas Laser & Aesthetics Training Academy. He treats removal as a gradual process rather than a rush, and works closely with each client to plan a staged removal path, whether the goal is to fade a piece for a cover-up or remove it completely.

"If it's visually striking, it captivates me."Minh
