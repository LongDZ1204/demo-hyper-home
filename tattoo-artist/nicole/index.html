More About Nicole

** "I feel genuine passion and enjoyment in my piercing work." (Nicole)

Background

Nicole's full name is Ngoc Yen Nhi Dinh, a Vietnamese artist with three years of experience in tattooing and piercing, and a notable female tattoo artist in San Antonio. Her expertise covers a wide range of body piercings, but her true passion lies in unique placements like snake eyes and navel piercings. Her journey began with an interest in tattooing, but the moment she picked up a piercing needle, it felt like destiny.

Craft Approach

While she still tattoos, Nicole has truly found her calling as a professional piercer, and her love for the craft deepens each day. At Hyper Inkers, she finds fulfillment in beautifying clients through her piercing work while holding an unwavering commitment to her responsibilities.

Specialization

Her gentle hands and quick movements define her approach, giving clients security and confidence during their sessions. Nicole combines technical precision with artistic vision to create a comfortable experience for anyone seeking tattoo and piercing services at Hyper Inkers.

Q&A With Nicole

What inspires you as an artist?

I feel genuine passion and enjoyment in my piercing work. This connection to the art form drives my creative process and fuels my dedication to each client's unique design.

Why should someone pick you as their artist?

Because I have gentle hands and quick movements, clients feel more secure and less worried during their sessions.
