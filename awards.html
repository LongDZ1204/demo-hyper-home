Best of Show

New York Empire State Tattoo Expo 2026 · New York City, NY

Minh Pham won Best of Show at the New York Empire State Tattoo Expo 2026, the highest honor of one of the most prestigious conventions in the United States. Held over three days at the Hilton Midtown, the event draws elite artists from around the world. For Minh, the win capped a decade of work and stands as one of the proudest moments in Hyper Inkers history.

Won by Minh · Founder & Lead Artist

Best of Show

San Antonio Tattoo Arts Festival 2025 · San Antonio, TX

Minh Pham won Best of Show at the San Antonio Tattoo Arts Festival 2025, the convention's top honor. The winning piece was a tribute for a client from Vietnam who named his daughter Panda. Minh turned that personal story into a detailed work of art that paired technical skill with real emotional weight.

Won by Minh · Founder & Lead Artist
